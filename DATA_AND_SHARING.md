# Data and sharing boundary

This public repository is code-only. Do not add collaborator-supplied raw VIBE measurements, proprietary/unpublished antibody sequences, Apoha internal files, unpublished experimental plans, manuscript drafts, unpublished result tables, DeepSP weights or other third-party model files unless the relevant owner has explicitly approved public release.

Academic authorship or access to a dataset does not automatically grant redistribution rights. Public release can also count as disclosure before manuscript submission. When in doubt, keep the file private and ask the supervisor/data owner in writing.

Public papers and public datasets should normally be cited or linked at their original source rather than re-uploaded here unless the licence clearly permits redistribution.
