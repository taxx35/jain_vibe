# Notice

This repository is a public, data-free research and portfolio artifact.

It excludes:

- collaboration datasets and assay measurements;
- restricted antibody sequence tables;
- private protein-language-model embeddings;
- unpublished manuscript figures;
- prospective mutation rankings and construct-selection materials;
- collaborator-sensitive metadata.

The synthetic benchmark is derived from a validation problem encountered in the research, but its synthetic sequences, family labels and target values are artificial. They should not be interpreted as biological findings.

No license is granted here for restricted collaboration material, because that material is not included.
